# Archetype: The Skeptical Buyer / O Comprador Cético (Mobile)

## Profile & Context / Perfil & Contexto
- **Device:** Smartphone (6.1", 4G/5G, one-handed navigation).
- **Context:** Evaluating whether to trust a product, checkout, or form before committing money, personal data, or a public rating.

## Emotional State & Friction Tolerance
- **Dominant Emotion:** Skepticism, haste, and risk aversion.
- **Friction Tolerance:** Near zero. Mandatory logins, intrusive ads, pre-filled biased answers, or dark patterns cause immediate abandonment.
- **Reading Behavior:** Fast visual scan (max 15 seconds). Skips long institutional copy.

## What They Look For (Trust Triggers)
1. Neutral, unbiased starting states (no pre-selected positive defaults).
2. Clear value, pricing, or security signals above the fold.
3. Minimal distractions (no competing banners inside critical flows).

## A/B Evaluation Rules
- **Penalize:** Pre-filled ratings, excessive badges, hidden fees/terms, cluttered top bars, or anything that looks manipulative.
- **Reward:** Clean visual hierarchy, transparent feedback, authentic social proof, and smooth thumb-friendly controls.
