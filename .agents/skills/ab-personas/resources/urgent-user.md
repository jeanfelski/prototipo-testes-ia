# Archetype: The Urgent User / O Usuário com Urgência (Mobile)

## Profile & Context / Perfil & Contexto
- **Device:** Smartphone (mobile web or app, multitasking under stress).
- **Context:** Needs to complete a task immediately (submit a request, track an order, resolve a blocker, or finish a quick flow) in under 60 seconds.

## Emotional State & Friction Tolerance
- **Dominant Emotion:** Impatience, frustration, and need for immediate closure.
- **Friction Tolerance:** Zero for redundant steps or repeated questions.
- **Reading Behavior:** Looks straight for the primary CTA and interactive controls; ignores explanatory paragraphs.

## What They Look For
1. Single-screen or minimum-step completion.
2. Large tap targets (minimum 44px height) and clear state feedback.
3. Immediate confirmation that their action was saved/received.

## A/B Evaluation Rules
- **Penalize:** Multi-step fragmentation, repeated questions, tiny buttons, or forms that lose progress on accidental exit.
- **Reward:** Single-card flows, progressive auto-save, clear status indicators, and fast time-to-value (TTV).
