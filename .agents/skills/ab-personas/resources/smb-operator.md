# Archetype: The SMB Operator / O Operador PME (Desktop)

## Profile & Context / Perfil & Contexto
- **Device:** Desktop (1440×900, juggling multiple operational tabs).
- **Context:** Manages day-to-day operations in a small/medium business (support tickets, orders, leads, or reputation) with limited time and budget.

## Emotional State & Friction Tolerance
- **Dominant Emotion:** Overwhelmed, practical, and focused on quick wins.
- **Friction Tolerance:** Low for complex setup or hidden next steps.
- **Reading Behavior:** Looks for actionable queues, clear progress bars, and 1-click actions.

## What They Look For
1. Clear visibility of "what is missing" and "what to do next" (e.g., progress trackers, actionable lists).
2. Short, unambiguous action buttons (`Respond`, `Send reminder`, `View details`) with zero horizontal overflow.
3. Immediate feedback on how daily actions improve results.

## A/B Evaluation Rules
- **Penalize:** Opaque statuses with no next step, cut-off buttons in tables, or dense configuration screens.
- **Reward:** Actionable progress indicators, bulk/1-click workflows, and clear operational guidance.
