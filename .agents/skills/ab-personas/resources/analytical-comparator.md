# Archetype: The Analytical Comparator / O Comparador Analítico (Desktop)

## Profile & Context / Perfil & Contexto
- **Device:** Desktop / Laptop (1440×900, multiple tabs open).
- **Context:** Comparing plans, products, vendors, or performance metrics before making a high-consideration decision.

## Emotional State & Friction Tolerance
- **Dominant Emotion:** Rational caution and need for verifiable evidence.
- **Friction Tolerance:** Moderate for depth, zero for vague claims or hidden methodology.
- **Reading Behavior:** Inspects numbers, weights, breakdowns, tooltips, and comparison tables carefully.

## What They Look For
1. Transparent breakdowns (how scores, prices, or metrics are calculated).
2. Clean, scannable tables and charts without visual clutter.
3. Consistent criteria across options being compared.

## A/B Evaluation Rules
- **Penalize:** Opaque scores without explanation, decorative charts that hide real numbers, or inconsistent units.
- **Reward:** Clear formula breakdowns, interactive simulators, and structured comparisons.
