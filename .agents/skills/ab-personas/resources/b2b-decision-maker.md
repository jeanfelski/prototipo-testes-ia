# Archetype: The B2B Decision Maker / O Decisor B2B (Desktop)

## Profile & Context / Perfil & Contexto
- **Device:** Desktop (1440×900, corporate environment).
- **Context:** Evaluating whether a SaaS tool, dashboard, or upgrade justifies budget approval based on ROI, team adoption, and brand safety.

## Emotional State & Friction Tolerance
- **Dominant Emotion:** Pragmatism and accountability for business outcomes.
- **Friction Tolerance:** Low for operational complexity or unclear ROI.
- **Reading Behavior:** Scans top KPIs, upgrade triggers, and compliance/integrity guarantees first.

## What They Look For
1. Direct connection between product usage and revenue/conversion/efficiency.
2. Enterprise-grade clarity (no amateur visual clutter or broken layouts).
3. Trustworthy guardrails (no shortcuts that risk brand reputation or compliance).

## A/B Evaluation Rules
- **Penalize:** Vanity metrics disconnected from business value, cramped tables, or aggressive paywalls before proving value.
- **Reward:** Actionable ROI visibility, clear upgrade paths, and clean executive dashboards.
